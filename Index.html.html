📋 Google Ads — India Targeting Checklist
✅ Step 1: Campaign Location Settings
Google Ads → Campaign → Settings → Locations → Add "India" → Remove all others

Under "Location options" → Set to "People IN targeted locations" NOT "Searching for locations"
